FAQ & Policies

 

How do I redeem a digital code?

Open your account dashboard, go to Orders, and press "Reveal code". Then enter it on the Steam, PlayStation Store, Xbox or Nintendo eShop redeem page of the matching region.

What warranty do used items have?

Used hardware has 7 days checking warranty and 1 month service warranty. New hardware has up to 12 months.

Can I return an item?

Yes, within 7 days if it is not as described. Opened digital codes and discs are not returnable.

Can I cancel a pre-order?

Yes, anytime before the item ships. You get a full refund within 5 working days.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

Yes, COD is available in major cities. Other options: JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer and cards.
